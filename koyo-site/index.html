OUR STORY — 이야기

Stillness is not emptiness. It is the blank canvas — a clear mind with room for the next stroke. We offer Korean teas as gentle reminders to pause, to breathe, and to return to yourself.

Founded in 2019 by a collective of Koreans and Korean-Americans, Koyo bridges a two-thousand-year tea tradition and the pace of modern life.

We came to tea from different paths — contemporary art, engineering, design, theology, agriculture, medicine — and converged on a single philosophy: stillness leads a healthier, more balanced life. Korea has long been celebrated for its natural beauty and serene traditions; we set out to carry that essence forward.

Our teas grow in Hadong, where mist settles between the ridges of Jirisan and tea has been cultivated since the ninth century. Every leaf is picked by hand from pesticide-free gardens, in small batches — quality over quantity, as the mountain has always demanded.

THE RITUAL — 손맛

Brewed by feel.

Korean tea culture trusts the drinker. Sohn-mat — “the taste of one’s hands” — is the belief that intuition seasons better than instruction. Our recommendations are a place to begin. Never law.

  1. 一

    Warm

    Bring mineral water just past the pearl-bubble point, then let it rest until the steam rises softly.

  2. 二

    Steep

    A single gram of leaves. One quiet minute. Pour slowly, and watch the color arrive.

  3. 三

    Return

    Steep again, and once more — two minutes, then four. Each cup reveals a different tea.
